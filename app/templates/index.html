How RAG Works

Grounded Document Question Answering

Every answer generated by this assistant is strictly grounded in the content of your indexed PDF documents. When you ask a question:

1
Dense Retrieval

Your question is converted into a vector embedding to search the most relevant chunks in ChromaDB.

2
Context Injection

The top matching excerpts are extracted alongside strict provenance (filename & page numbers).

3
Grounded Generation

The local Ollama LLM formulates a factual answer restricted solely to the retrieved context.

Answers cite exact PDF filenames and page numbers. Unverifiable questions are explicitly acknowledged.
